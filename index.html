How to read this

Critical miss rate is the share of genuinely malicious alerts a model dismissed as benign or a false positive, or rated informational or low. This is the number that matters most. A missed intrusion is the failure a SOC exists to prevent, so the table is sorted by it and a zero here means the model let nothing through on this set.

False alarms are false-positive alerts the model escalated as high or critical. They measure the opposite failure: a tool that cries wolf gets ignored.

Score combines verdict, severity, recommended action and the quality of the written rationale, weighted 40, 20, 25 and 15 percent, against an expert's reference decision. The interval is a 95 percent bootstrap over examples; where intervals overlap, the ordering is not meaningful.

Every example is synthetic. Alerts, hosts, people and indicators are invented and use reserved address ranges and example domains. Reference decisions were written or reviewed by a security practitioner. Rationales are scored by an LLM judge that sees only the reference evidence and the rationale.
